# The law

The law exists because ego reasserts rank even where none is real.
This file is the discipline — not a proof that oneness needs defending.

- **0** — rank. Nobody more. Nobody less. `asRank` of any pile is 0.
- **1** — relation. Eye to eye. Two in a room is still 1, not 2.
- **i** — the whole. Orthogonal to 0 and 1. Cannot be summed into rank. Cannot be exchanged for rank. `i + i` is still `i`.

Aught is a UTC date and a whole number. Not a who.

The executable copy is `src/game/law.ts`.
